## Practical publishing sequence

1. Confirm handles and owner access; refresh profile, logo, description and website.
2. Publish the launch post, then the useful resource and product example over the
   following week when someone can answer replies. Cadence is a working plan,
   not an algorithm or ranking claim.
3. Answer interested clinics with the pilot page; do not request patient records
   in public comments or direct messages.
4. Record qualified conversations and pilots in a simple manual log. On the site,
   contact_form_submit means attempt; generate_lead means Formspree accepted it,
   not that the clinic qualified or started. Analytics requires visitor consent.
5. Review which post generated substantive conversations before increasing volume.

No social followers, testimonials, clinical outcomes or time savings are claimed.
Social presence is a distribution and trust experiment; it does not guarantee SEO rankings.
